Sounds

Your sound-effect library. Claude picks from these when it suggests sounds for an edit, using each sound's description, so write when to use it. Every sound is cleaned up as it's added: silence at the start and end is cut (it plays exactly at its marker) and it's levelled to about the loudness of normal talking.

Ideas to collect, and where to find them

Reactions: FAAAH (fail scream), vine boom, bruh, "oh no" (Kreepa), sad violin / trombone (wah wah wah), record scratch, "emotional damage", Windows XP error, Windows shutdown, cartoon "bonk", anime "nani?!", "hello darkness my old friend" piano sting, crickets, awkward cough.

Hits and timing: whoosh, swoosh, impact boom, punch, slap, glass break, explosion, camera shutter, ding (correct), buzzer (wrong), cash register, pop, notification ping.

Build-ups: riser, suspense drone, drum roll, heartbeat, tense violin, dramatic "dun dun dunnn".

Safer sources (free to use): freesound.org (filter by CC0), pixabay.com/sound-effects, the YouTube Audio Library (sound effects tab). Note the license in "Source and license".

Meme sounds are often cut from TV shows, films or games. Short ones rarely get a Content ID claim, but they can. Note where each came from, so you know which ones to swap if a clip gets claimed.

Good descriptions say the meaning and the moment, not just the sound: "bonk: someone says something dumb or gets corrected", "riser: 2 s before a big reveal or a jump scare".
